What we do and how we work

From registration to the test drive

We take care of test and prototype vehicles, including pre-homologation vehicles: we register and insure them in Poland, instrument them and run the test drives in Poland and across Europe. One partner for the vehicle and the drive.

  1. 01

    Scope

    You tell us which vehicles, what the test programme needs and when.

  2. 02

    Registration and insurance

    We register test and prototype vehicles in Poland, including pre-homologation vehicles that do not yet have type approval, and provide their insurance.

  3. 03

    Instrumentation

    We install, check and look after the measurement equipment the test programme needs, e.g. data loggers, sensors and cameras.

  4. 04

    Test drives

    We run the test drives on test tracks and on public roads, in Poland and across Europe, and analyse the results.
