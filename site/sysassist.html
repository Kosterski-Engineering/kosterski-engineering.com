What it is

An assistant, not a replacement

SysAssist works directly in your ALM. It decomposes stakeholder requirements into system requirement drafts, reviews the requirements you already have, and drafts test cases from what you approve.

Every suggestion waits for an engineer’s decision, and every decision is recorded as a comment on the item itself. Nothing is written to your ALM without approval.

Where it works in the V

From customer requirement to test case draft

On the left side of the V it drafts and reviews, down to the architecture. On the right side it drafts test cases from approved requirements. Software and hardware engineering are not in scope.

SYS.1

Requirements elicitation

Consolidates stakeholder requirements scattered across documents and drafts clarifying questions where the intent is unclear.

SYS.2

System requirements

Decomposes stakeholder requirements into system requirement drafts with trace links, and checks existing ones for completeness, unambiguity and verifiability.

SYS.3

System architecture

Proposes the allocation of system requirements to architecture elements and flags interfaces that are not yet defined.

SYS.4 · SYS.5

Test case drafts

Drafts test cases from approved requirements and interfaces, each traced back to its source.

Stays with you

The engineer decides

  • Approving every change before it reaches your ALM
  • Architecture decisions: it proposes options, you choose
  • Talking to the customer: questions come to you first
  • Closing a requirement as verified

First step

A short, paid pilot

A pilot on a narrow task you choose, with success criteria agreed in writing before the start. Before the pilot we record your baseline; afterwards we compare on the same kind of work.

The connection to your ALM starts read-only. Your process does not change until you decide it should.
