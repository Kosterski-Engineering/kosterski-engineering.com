1. Controller

The controller of personal data processed in connection with this website is Kosterski Engineering sp. z o.o., Wyspa Słodowa 7, 50-266 Wrocław, Poland, VAT ID: PL8982307447. Contact for data protection matters:
contact@kosterski-engineering.com.

We have not appointed a data protection officer.

This policy explains how we process personal data of website visitors, people who contact us, candidates in our recruitment processes and our business contacts.

2. Visiting the website

The website is hosted by Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., with its EU entity Cloudflare Ltd.). When you visit a page, Cloudflare technically processes your IP address and request data (date and time, requested page, browser and operating system) to deliver the page and protect it from attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA; Cloudflare, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

3. Cookies, analytics and external content

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and loads no content from third parties. Fonts are served from our own server; the website does not connect to Google Fonts.

Links to other websites only take effect when you click them. The privacy policy of the respective site applies there.

This policy also covers the SysAssist product pages on this website.

4. Contact by e-mail

If you write to us, we process the data in your message (for example name, e-mail address, company and content) to reply and to discuss a possible cooperation (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We keep the correspondence for as long as the conversation requires and afterwards for the limitation period of possible claims or as long as statutory retention obligations apply (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

5. Recruitment

We recruit engineers for our own projects and for projects we carry out for our clients.

Where we get your data. Directly from you (for example your CV, conversations and correspondence) or from publicly available professional sources: profiles on networks such as LinkedIn or XING, job portals, company websites, and recommendations from other people.

What data we process. Your name, contact details, location, career history, education, skills and languages, availability, expectations regarding cooperation, links to your professional profiles, your CV and notes from recruitment conversations. We do not collect special categories of data (for example health data), and we do not need your photo or date of birth.

Purposes and legal bases.

  • Searching for candidates and contacting you for the first time with a job or project opportunity – our legitimate interest in finding qualified engineers (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Running a recruitment process you take part in – steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for employment contracts under Polish law also Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in connection with Art. 22¹ of the Polish Labour Code.
  • Presenting your profile to our client – your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). We ask for it before we share your CV with a specific client.
  • Contacting you about future opportunities – your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims – our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

How long we keep your data. For the duration of the recruitment process and then for 6 months in case of claims. If you consent to being contacted about future opportunities – until you withdraw your consent, but no longer than 2 years from when you gave it. If we contacted you based on your public profile and you did not reply – no longer than 3 months after our first message. If you tell us you are not interested, we delete your data and keep only a minimal note so that we do not contact you again.

6. Business contacts

We process data of people we work with, or would like to work with, at our clients, partners and prospective clients.

Where we get your data. Directly from you (for example business cards, meetings and correspondence), from the company you work for, or from publicly available professional sources such as LinkedIn, XING and company websites.

What data we process. Your name, business contact details, job title, employer, a link to your professional profile and information from our correspondence and meetings.

Purposes and legal bases. Establishing and maintaining business relationships, including presenting our services, and performing contracts with the company you represent – our legitimate interest in running and developing our business (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims – our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We send commercial information by e-mail, or call you for marketing purposes, only where the law allows it, in particular with your consent.

How long we keep your data. Until you object, but no longer than 2 years after our last contact. Data related to concluded contracts – for the limitation period of possible claims and for as long as statutory retention obligations apply.

7. Service providers and transfers outside the EEA

We use the following service providers, who process data on our behalf under data processing agreements:

  • Cloudflare – hosting of this website (see section 2),
  • Microsoft (Microsoft 365) – e-mail, documents and file storage,
  • Anthropic (Claude) – our internal tools, including our CRM for business contacts and recruitment.

In recruitment, we share your data with our clients only with your consent.

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the USA. Such transfers are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission. You can request information about these safeguards at contact@kosterski-engineering.com.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification (Art. 16 GDPR) and erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR), where we process your data based on consent or a contract,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR); you can object to processing for direct marketing purposes at any time, and we will then stop such processing,
  • withdraw your consent at any time; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.

To exercise your rights, write to contact@kosterski-engineering.com. We will reply within one month.

You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your residence or place of work. In Poland, this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

9. Automated decision-making

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you. IT tools, including AI, may support our recruiters, but decisions are made by people.

10. Changes

We update this policy when the way the website works changes. The date of the last change is shown at the top.
