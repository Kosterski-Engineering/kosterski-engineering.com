The situation

Prototype vehicles change every week

Test and prototype vehicles are rebuilt, reflashed, shipped between sites and shared by several teams. Their status often lives in spreadsheets, e-mails and the heads of a few people. When a test campaign needs a vehicle in a known configuration, finding out what is actually built into it takes time.
